The next morning, Elsa woke up Anna saying she now had a
surprise for her. They walked over to the window.

“How did this get here?” Anna asked, delighted by a freshly
iced pond.

“Well, since we were having too much fun planning things,”
said Elsa, “which is what Mother would have wanted, I
decided a bit of magic was in order, after all.”

“Mother would be so proud,” Anna said. “Now
let’s go kick off the festival!”

“After you,” said Elsa.
