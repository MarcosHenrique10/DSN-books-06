Together, Olaf and Anna went to look for Queen Elsa.
 They found her in the Great Hall.

“There you are, Elsa!” Olaf cried out joyfully.

Olaf looked up shyly at the dignitary standing with
 Elsa. “Hi, my name is Olaf and I like warm hugs.”

“H-h-hello,” the dignitary stammered in surprise.
He had never seen a talking snowman before.
