Meanwhile, Elsa had escaped from the dungeon, but
 Hans was close behind her. “Elsa, you can’t run from
 this,” said Hans. He told Elsa about her magic blast to
 Anna’s heart. “I tried to save her, but it was too late.”

Elsa collapsed in the snow and closed her eyes.
 Everything she had done to protect her sister had failed.
 It was all her fault.

Nearby, Anna was hurrying toward Kristoff, when she
 heard the clang of Hans’s sword. She turned and saw her
 sister—Elsa was in danger!
