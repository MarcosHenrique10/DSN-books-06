Hans left Anna alone and shivering. Luckily, Olaf found
 her and helped her warm up by the fire. But Anna was still
 getting weaker and weaker.

As Anna told him about Hans’s evil plan, Olaf glanced
 out the window and saw Kristoff racing toward the castle. He
 realized that Kristoff loved Anna. “There’s your act of true love
 right there!” Olaf told her. It was Kristoff whom Anna needed
 to kiss! With the last of her strength, Anna struggled outside.
