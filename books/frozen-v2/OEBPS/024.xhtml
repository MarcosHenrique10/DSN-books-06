Anna’s hair was turning snowy white!

“It’s because she struck you, isn’t it?” said Kristoff. Right
 away, he brought Anna to the trolls, hoping they could help.
 One troll told them that Elsa’s icy magic had struck Anna’s
 heart. If the magic was not reversed, Anna would soon be frozen
 solid. Only an act of true love could thaw a frozen heart.

Anna knew she loved Hans—maybe a kiss from him would
 work! As the friends hurried toward Arendelle, Anna began to
 shiver. Kristoff was especially worried about her. He was starting
 to care for Anna.
