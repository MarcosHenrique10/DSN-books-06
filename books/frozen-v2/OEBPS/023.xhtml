Elsa used her magic to create a huge snowman. He chased
 the friends out of the palace and toward a tall cliff.

Kristoff pulled out a rope to help them climb down.

“What if we fall?” asked Anna.

“There’s twenty feet of fresh powder down there,” said
 Kristoff. “It will be like landing on a pillow . . . hopefully.”

They leaped over the edge and landed safely on the fluffy
 snow below. They had escaped from the snowman, but Anna
 had other things to worry about. . . .
