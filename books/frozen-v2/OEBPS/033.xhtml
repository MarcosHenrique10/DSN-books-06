“Oh, Anna. No. Please, no!” said Elsa, clutching her
 sister. Suddenly, Anna began to thaw! Her arms, warm again,
 reached around Elsa, and the two sisters hugged.

As Olaf watched them, he remembered what the wise old
 troll had said: “An act of true love will thaw a frozen heart.”
 Anna’s love for Elsa had saved them—and the kingdom.
