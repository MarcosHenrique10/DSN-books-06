When Anna arrived in Arendelle, she said good-bye to
 Kristoff and Olaf. Then she raced to see Hans.

As soon as they were alone, Anna asked Hans to save
 her with a kiss. But Hans refused! Anna realized that he
 had only pretended to love her. He wanted to take over
 Arendelle by getting rid of Anna and Elsa! “All that’s left
 now is to kill Elsa and bring back summer,” said Hans.
