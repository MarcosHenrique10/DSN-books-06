Anna tried over and over again to convince Elsa to come
 home. But Elsa was too scared that she would hurt more
 people. As Elsa argued with her sister, an icy wave of magic
 burst from her body—and struck Anna in the chest!

“Anna!” Kristoff cried out as he charged inside the palace.

Anna stood up and looked at Elsa. “No. I’m not leaving
 without you, Elsa,” she told her.

“Yes, you are,” said Elsa. She knew what she had to do.
