It was a beautiful spring day. The sun was shining over
 Arendelle as Anna bounced around the kitchen. She had
 planned a picnic for all her best friends and couldn’t
 wait to get started.

“Are you ready?” Elsa asked, peeking into the kitchen.

“Almost.” Anna closed the lid to the picnic basket. “Most of
 the bags are in the hall, but can you grab that brown one?”
