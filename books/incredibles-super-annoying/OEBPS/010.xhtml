Dash jumped up wearing some of Violet’s clothes. “Look at
 me. I’m Violet. I’m in love with Tony Rydinger,” he teased.

Violet grabbed him and dragged him out of her room. “Stay
out!” she yelled, slamming the door.

Dash dropped the clothes in the hall, sped out the front
 door, and snuck into the backyard. He stood on tiptoe to peer
 through Violet’s window. “Yoo-hoo, Violet!” he called with a
 dainty wave.
