Violet rushed to the window and yanked down the
 shade. As she sat on her bed, she felt something under her.
 “Aaaahhh!” she screamed.

“Forget to lock something?” Dash said, poking his head out
 from under the covers.

 Super speed that all Violet saw was a blur kicking up papers
 and blowing the posters off her wall.

“Dash!” Violet screamed. “Knock it off!”
