That was it! Violet had had enough! “Give
 that back!” she yelled.

Dash started to run, but Violet threw a
 force field in front of the door. Dash ran into
 it head on and was knocked to the floor.

The diary fell out of Dash’s hand, and
 Violet snatched it away. But before she knew
 it, the diary flew away in a sudden whirlwind.
“‘How dost I love thee?’” Dash read from
across the room.

Violet turned invisible and lunged for
 Dash. “You’re gonna get it!” she cried, and
 they chased each other around her room in a
 storm of Super powers.

Suddenly, they heard their mom call:
 “Violet! Dash! Dinner time!”
