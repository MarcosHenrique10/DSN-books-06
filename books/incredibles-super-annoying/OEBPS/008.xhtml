Things were not the way she’d left them. The bedspread
 was upside down. The lampshade was gone. Her books had
 been moved. And her posters were rearranged on her wall.
 “Mom! Dash messed with my stuff again!” Violet hollered.

As Helen walked down the hall, a breeze whipped through
 Violet’s room. Helen looked inside. “It looks fine to me, honey,”
 she said.

Violet’s eyes scanned the room. Everything
 was back in place!
