Suddenly, Mabel floated into the air.
She was glowing and her eyes
were white. “Welcome to your
graves, young trespassers!”
boomed Mabel in a deep
otherworldly voice.
“Welcome to your home
for all eternity!”

Then everything in
the store floated into
the air, and the ceiling
became the floor.

“What does it want from us?” Wendy
screamed as she and Dipper dove into
a cabinet.

“Okay, let’s try to figure out the
pattern here,” Dipper said. “Why was
each person taken?”
