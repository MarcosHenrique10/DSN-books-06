When the royal grooms were done with Sven, he positively
 shone!

“Sven,” Anna said, “you look Svendid!” She elbowed Elsa.
 “Get it? Svendid?”

“I get it,” Elsa said with a smile. “You really do look
 magnificent, Sven.” Then she frowned. “But I think there’s
 something missing.”

Elsa hung a huge flower wreath around Sven’s neck.

“There,” she said. “Now you’re perfect.”
