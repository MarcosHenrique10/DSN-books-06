Anna and Elsa went to search the royal wardrobe.

“How about this?” Anna asked, putting a silly hat on Elsa.

Elsa giggled and held up some boots. “These are definitely
 you,” she told Anna.

The sisters picked wilder and wilder outfits for each other.
 Soon they were laughing so hard they could hardly stand.

“Okay, it’s time to get serious,” Elsa said. “You need
 something special to wear to the parade!”
