With a little help from Anna’s friends, the parade was
 shaping up beautifully. It was going to be exactly like when
 Anna and Elsa were kids! Well . . . almost exactly.

“I don’t think you can ride Mister Waffles in the parade,”
 Elsa told Anna. “You’re bigger than he is now. Besides, I’m
 pretty sure he’s retired.”

“Then I’ll have to find a new horse!” Anna said. “The best
 horse in all of Arendelle.”
