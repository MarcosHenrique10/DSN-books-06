Anna introduced Sven to the royal stables’ grooms. “He’s
 going to be leading the parade with me,” she explained. “So he
 needs to look extra fancy.”

“It’s an honor,” the head groom said, bowing low. “Please
 come with us, sir.”

The grooms set to work on Sven. They oiled his hooves.
 They polished his antlers. And they brushed and brushed and
 brushed his fur.
