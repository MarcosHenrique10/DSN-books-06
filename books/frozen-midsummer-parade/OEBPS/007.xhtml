“We haven’t had a midsummer parade since we were little
 kids,” Anna said sadly.

Elsa nodded. “Now that the gates are open, we should have
 it again. Starting this year!”

Anna clapped her hands. “You’ll look so great at the head
 of the parade,” she told her sister.

Elsa grinned slyly. “Not me. You! I hereby declare you
 Midsummer Princess.”
