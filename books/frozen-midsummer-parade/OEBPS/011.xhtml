Anna and Olaf headed to
 the stable to find the right horse.

“What about that one?” Anna
 asked the head groom, pointing
 at an elegant mare.

“She’s so pretty!” Olaf sighed
 admiringly.

“This is Lady Crystalbrook
 Shinytoes the Fourth,” the head
 groom said.

Lady Crystalbrook Shinytoes
 the Fourth stepped toward
 Anna . . . and tripped over
 her own feet. She fell right
 into the pond!

“Oh, dear,” Anna said.
