Elsa grinned. “I love midsummer,” she said. “Remember
 when we were kids and I used to lead—”

“The midsummer parade!” Anna interrupted, finishing
 her sister’s sentence. The midsummer parade was one of her
 happiest childhood memories.

“I loved that parade,” Anna told her sister. “You always
 looked so fancy, riding at the head of it.”

“On that fat little pony,” Elsa said with a chuckle. “Mister
 Waffles.”
