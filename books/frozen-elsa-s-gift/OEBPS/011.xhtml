“Kristoff!” Elsa called
 as she spotted her sister’s
 friend crossing the castle
 courtyard. “I need your
 help.”

Elsa explained her plan
 to create the perfect gift for
 Anna. “I thought you and I
 could make an ice sculpture
 for her,” she said.

But Elsa had more
 planned. While Kristoff
 went to get a block of ice,
 Elsa locked herself in the
 kitchen.

“Elsa?” Anna called. She
 knocked on the door. “Are
 you in there?”

“Don’t come in!” Elsa
 said. “It’s a surprise.”
