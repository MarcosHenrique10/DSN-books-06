When Elsa was sure
 Anna was gone, she tiptoed
 into her bedroom. Elsa’s
 closet was overflowing with
 trinkets she had collected
 for her sister over the
 years. Gathering them in
 her arms, she brought the
 presents to the banquet
 room. Elsa was arranging
 the gifts for her sister when
 Kristoff arrived with a giant
 block of ice.

Elsa wasted no time.
 Soon she had created a
 beautiful sculpture of
Anna.
