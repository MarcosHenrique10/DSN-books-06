So Olaf and Elsa got to work making Anna’s favorite—extra-gingery
 gingerbread men. The cookies turned out perfectly, and Elsa only had
 to refreeze Olaf seven times.

“Elsa?” Anna said, knocking on the door again. “It smells great in there!
 Can I help?”

“Nope!” Elsa called back. “I’ll see you later. Just . . . go to your room.”
