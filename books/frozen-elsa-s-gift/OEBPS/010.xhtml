But Elsa wasn’t paying attention. She was
 thinking about the ball.

“Earth to Elsa,” Anna called.

Elsa looked at her sister and smiled. Anna’s act
 of love had saved Arendelle—and Elsa. Elsa wished she
 could do something special for Anna—something to
 show everyone how much she loved her sister.

Looking around the banquet room, Elsa had an
 idea. “I, um . . . I have to go,” she told Anna, and
 raced out the door.
