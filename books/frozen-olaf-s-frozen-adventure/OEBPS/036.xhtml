GLOSSARY

bordstabelbakkels (BOO-stahbel-beckls)—table
stacking cookies.

kransekake (krun-suh-KA-kay)—a wreath cake
made out of rings

lefse (le-fs)—a traditional Scandinavian
flatbread, usually made around the holidays.

Yule—rhymes with mule

tomte (TOM-t)—a mythological creature from
Scandinavian folklore typically associated with
the winter season.
