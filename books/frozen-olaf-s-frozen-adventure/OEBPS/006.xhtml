The castle courtyard was filled
 with festive townspeople. Everyone
 had been excited as Kristoff and
 Sven brought in the Yule Bell.

“The Yule Bell signals the start
 of the holidays in Arendelle!” Elsa
 told Olaf.

“Okay, now,” Anna whispered.

Bong! Bong! Bong! The bell rang
 out, and all the villagers cheered!
