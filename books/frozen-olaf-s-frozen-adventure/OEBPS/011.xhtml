With the villagers gone, the sisters needed cheering up.
 Kristoff serenaded them with his holiday tradition from the
 trolls, “The Ballad of Flemingrad.” But the song took
 a strange turn when Kristof started singing about nostrils.

Then Kristoff revealed another troll tradition: Flemmy
 the Fungus Troll!

“Woah, gross,” said Anna.

“Now you lick his forehead and make a wish!” Kristoff said.

Everyone laughed.
