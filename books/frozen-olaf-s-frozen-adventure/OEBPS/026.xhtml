Anna and Elsa headed into the forest, calling out
 Olaf’s name. Kristoff and Sven were close behind, with a
 search party of villagers.

“Olaf, where are you?” wondered a worried Anna.

Just when they thought they might never find him, the
 sisters spotted a carrot sticking out of a snowdrift—Olaf!

Olaf explained how he had lost all the traditions he’d
 collected for his friends. Even the fruitcake, which had
 been grabbed by a bird!
