Olaf knocked on the door of the first house they came to.

“What is your holiday tradition?” Olaf asked a young boy
 and his mother.

“We make candy canes together.” The boy handed one
 to Olaf.

Olaf pulled out his carrot nose and popped in the candy
 cane. His eyes whirled. “Ohhh, sugar rush!”

The boy stared at Olaf. “You’re supposed to eat it.”

“Eat my new nose? Why would I do that?” asked Olaf.

“Because it’s that time of year!” the boy said.
