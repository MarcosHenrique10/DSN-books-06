“Snow angels!” the baroness cried out. “I love
 snow angels. What a delightful surprise!”

“I say,” the baron chuckled. “The kingdom

 please its visitors!”

Soon everyone was enjoying the snow and the
 décor. The king and queen could not have been
 more delighted to see their visitors having so
 much fun. All the guests wanted to do business

 and sophisticated kingdom.
