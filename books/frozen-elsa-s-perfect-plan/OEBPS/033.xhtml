Just then, there was a light knock at the door. It was Anna.

“Um—I can’t open the door now,” Elsa said. She didn’t
 want Anna to see any of the gifts.

“Elsa?” Anna asked. “You know it’s my birthday
 tomorrow, right?”

“Of course,” Elsa said. “Would you like to do something
 together? I’ve cleared the whole day.”

“I’d love that,” Anna said. “See you bright and early? But
 maybe not too early . . .” Anna was not a morning person.
