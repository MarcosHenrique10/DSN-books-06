One day, he told his friends Anna and
Kristoff about his dream and they couldn’t
help but smile. “No matter the season,”
said Anna, “we’ll be by your side.”

“Yeah,” said Kristoff, “even when
summer does turn you into a—”

“Puddles are fun to splash in, aren’t
they?” interrupted Olaf.

Anna and Kristoff could only nod,
letting their friend dream big.
