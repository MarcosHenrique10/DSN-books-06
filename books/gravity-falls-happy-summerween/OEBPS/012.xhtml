Back at the Mystery Shack, Dipper reluctantly
put on his costume.

“Introducing, for the first time in public . . .
peanut butter and jelly!” announced Mabel.

“Aww,” said Soos, Candy, and Grenda
in unison.

“Let’s just get this over with,” said Dipper.

Mabel was right! The twins’ costumes melted
the hearts of everyone in Gravity Falls.
