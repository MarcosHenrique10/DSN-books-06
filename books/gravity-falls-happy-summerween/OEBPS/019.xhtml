“We have to hide!” said Dipper. “Everyone stay quiet.”

“Now you’re worried about the monster? I thought all
you cared about was Wendy,” Mabel whispered to Dipper.

“You know that’s not true,” said Dipper. “I just felt
like I was getting a little too old to go trick- or- treating.”

“That’s exactly why we need to go trick- or- treating!
We’re getting older. There’s not that many Summerweens
left.” Mabel looked sadder than he’d ever seen her.
