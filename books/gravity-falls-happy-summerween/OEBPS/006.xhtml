As Dipper was throwing out the candy,
the doorbell rang. When he opened the
door, there stood Wendy and Robbie.

“Hey, what’s with the candy?” Robbie
asked him. “You going trick- or- treating?”

“No, uh . . . trick- or-
treating is for babies!”
said Dipper nervously,
trying to impress Wendy.

“Come to a cool party
with us,” Wendy told him.

“Sure!” Dipper
said. “I’ll see you
at the party!”
