Mabel’s friends Candy and Grenda
were ready to go trick- or- treating.

“Just wait until you see Dipper’s
costume!” Mabel told them.

But when Dipper showed,
he wasn’t wearing one.

“What the hey- hey,
bro- bro? Where’s your
costume?” Mabel asked.

“I can’t go trick- or- treating.
I’m . . . really sick,” Dipper said.
