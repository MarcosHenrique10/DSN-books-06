“I left it behind this bush,” said
Dipper. But behind the bush was a
deep ditch. The candy had fallen
into it and was floating in a
mucky puddle!

That’s when the Summerween
Trickster appeared.

“So, children . . . where’s
my candy?” he asked.

“We had it all, I swear!
It’s down there . . .
somewhere. We can
still get it!” said
Dipper.
